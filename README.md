# dashboards
[Experiments Retrospective](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/anna.kowalevskaia/viz/Experimentsoverview/ExperimentsRetrospective) - a dashboard for analyzing A/B testing results and experimentation health. The project helps teams monitor experiment outcomes, evaluate statistical reliability, detect traffic imbalance and experiment interference, and assess business impact through target and guardrail metrics. The dashboard improves visibility into experimentation processes and supports data-driven rollout decisions.<br>
[Sales Performance](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/anna.kowalevskaia/viz/SalesPerformancewithextension/SalesPerformance) - an enterprise commercial dashboard for analyzing sales pipeline dynamics, revenue performance, and team productivity. The project helps leadership and revenue operations monitor lead-to-deal conversion funnels, track Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) health, evaluate individual manager efficiency, and analyze market distribution across industries and geographies.
